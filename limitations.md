# Limitations and Validation Requirements

## Current Limitations

1. **Unvalidated scoring rules:** The factor scales and assignment criteria have not been established as validated measurement procedures.
2. **Subjectivity:** Different assessors may assign different scores to the same evidence.
3. **Factor dependence:** Reliability, corroboration, and provenance may overlap.
4. **Multiplicative behavior:** A low score for one factor can sharply reduce the composite.
5. **Context sensitivity:** Freshness requirements vary across topics and use cases.
6. **False precision:** Decimal scores can appear more exact than the underlying judgments justify.
7. **No truth probability:** The composite is not a calibrated probability that a claim is true.

## Potential Validation Steps

- Develop explicit scoring rubrics.
- Test whether independent assessors reach similar scores.
- Examine sensitivity to changes in each factor.
- Compare the multiplicative model with alternative approaches.
- Evaluate performance on appropriate datasets with defined reference criteria.
- Document failure cases and revise the framework transparently.

## Permitted Interpretation

Until validation is completed, scores should be treated as exploratory summaries that support structured review, not as definitive rankings of truth or substitutes for expert judgment.

**Status: Exploratory — not validated.**
