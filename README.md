# Evidence Quality Framework

## Overview

The Evidence Quality Framework is an exploratory research project of the Anthological Institute for Research & Governance.

It proposes a multiplicative composite for organizing four evidence-quality dimensions:

\[
Q_e = q_s \times q_t \times q_c \times q_p
\]

The dimensions are source reliability, freshness, corroboration, and provenance/completeness.

## Research Status

**Exploratory — not validated.**

The formula and example scores are proposed for methodological discussion. The composite is not a probability of truth or a validated predictor of evidence accuracy.

## Contribution and Review

Future revisions should document scoring criteria, supporting evidence, validation procedures, and changes to assumptions. Claims of validity should be supported by appropriate empirical results.
